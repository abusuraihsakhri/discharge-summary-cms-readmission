LACE readmission index

Calculate the published 0–19 point LACE index using length of stay, acuity of admission, comorbidity, and emergency department visits.

Research / educational use only. This is not the CMS Hospital-Wide All-Cause Readmission (HWR) measure, a predicted readmission percentage, or a validated clinical decision support service. Do not use it for treatment or discharge decisions.

Index inputs

Enter aggregate scores only; do not enter patient names, identifiers, or other protected health information.

0 indicates a stay of less than one day.

Use the comorbidity scoring specification from the original LACE study; this tool does not calculate Charlson components.

Submit the form to calculate and display a score. Results are calculated locally in this browser.

Methods and limitations

Point mapping follows Table 3 of van Walraven et al. (CMAJ, 2010). This calculator does not analyze discharge summaries, medication reconciliation gaps, clinical claims, or CMS risk-standardized hospital metrics. It is a standalone educational tool.

Privacy: no cookies, analytics, external APIs, or network submission of form data are used by this page. No patient-level data should be entered.
